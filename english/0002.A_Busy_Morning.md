# A Busy Morning

Jack gets up at six thirty every morning. He washes his face and brushes his teeth.

At seven o'clock, he makes breakfast for his family. He makes some eggs and a cup of coffee. His wife prepares some bread.

After breakfast, Jack checks his phone. He has three new messages from his coworkers. One message is about a meeting at nine o'clock.

Jack puts his laptop in his bag and leaves home at eight. He usually takes the bus to work, but today he rides his bike because the weather is nice.

When Jack arrives at the office, he says hello to his coworkers. He turns on his computer and starts working.

It is a busy morning, but Jack feels ready for the day.
